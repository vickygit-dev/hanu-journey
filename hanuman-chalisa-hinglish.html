Hanuman Chalisa Lyrics in English (Hinglish)

|| Atha Shree Hanuman Chalisa ||

Jai Shri Ram

॥Doha॥

Shri Guru Charan Saroj Raj
Nij Mane Mukuru Sudhari,
Varnao Raghuvar Vimal Jasu
Jo Dayaku Phal Char.

Meaning:

Having cleansed the mirror of my mind with the sacred dust of my Guru's lotus feet, I now sing the spotless glory of Lord Rama, whose blessings lead to the four aims of life: Dharma (righteousness), Artha (prosperity), Kama (fulfilment of desires) and Moksha (liberation).

Budhi Hin Tanu Janike
Sumirau Pavan Kumar,
Bal Buddhi Vidya Dehu Mohe
Harahu Kalesh Vikar.

Meaning:

Knowing that my knowledge and understanding are limited, I call upon Lord Hanuman, the divine son of the Wind God. Please grant me courage, intelligence and wisdom, and help me overcome my difficulties, suffering and negative tendencies.
Jai Shri Ram

॥Chaupai॥

Jai Hanuman Gyan Gun Sagar
Jai Kapis Tihun Lok Ujagar

Meaning:

Salutations to Lord Hanuman, the boundless source of wisdom and virtuous qualities. Glory to the mighty leader of the Vanaras, whose fame and greatness illuminate all three worlds.

Ram Doot Atulit Bal Dhama
Anjani Putra Pavan Sut Nama

Meaning:

You are the devoted messenger of Lord Rama and the dwelling place of immeasurable strength. Born to Mother Anjani, you are also honoured as Pavanputra, the son of the Wind God.

Mahavir Vikram Bajrangi
Kumati Nivar Sumati Ke Sangi

Meaning:

O mighty and fearless warrior, whose body possesses the strength of a thunderbolt! You drive away misguided thinking and encourage wisdom, clarity and good judgement.

Kanchan Varan Viraj Subesa
Kanan Kundal Kunchit Kesa

Meaning:

Your appearance shines with a beautiful golden radiance, and you wear magnificent garments. Your ears are adorned with earrings, while your curly hair adds to your divine appearance.

Hath Vajra Aur Dhuvaje Viraje
Kandhe Moonj Janehu Sajey

Meaning:

A thunderbolt weapon and a sacred flag adorn your hands, while a holy thread made from Munja grass rests across your shoulder.

Sankar Suvan Kesri Nandan
Tej Pratap Maha Jag Vandan

Meaning:

You are regarded as the divine manifestation of Lord Shiva and the beloved son of Kesari. Your extraordinary brilliance, strength and glory are honoured throughout the world.

Vidyavan Guni Ati Chatur
Ram Kaj Karibe Ko Aatur

Meaning:

You are richly endowed with learning, excellent virtues and remarkable intelligence. You are always enthusiastic and ready to undertake any responsibility that serves Lord Rama.

Prabu Charitra Sunibe Ko Rasiya
Ram Lakhan Sita Man Basiya

Meaning:

You find immense happiness in hearing the divine stories and deeds of Lord Rama. Your heart is forever devoted to Rama, Lakshmana and Mother Sita, who always reside within it.

Sukshma Roop Dhari Siyahi Dikhava
Vikat Roop Dhari Lanka Jarava

Meaning:

You took a tiny and gentle form when appearing before Mother Sita, but later assumed a terrifying and powerful appearance to set the city of Lanka ablaze.

Bhima Roop Dhari Asur Sanghare
Ramachandra Ke Kaj Sanvare

Meaning:

Taking on a formidable and mighty appearance, you defeated the demonic forces and successfully completed the missions entrusted to you by Lord Ramachandra.

Laye Sanjivan Lakhan Jiyaye
Shri Raghuvir Harashi Ur Laye

Meaning:

You brought the life-restoring Sanjeevani herb and saved Lakshmana's life. Overwhelmed with happiness, Lord Rama embraced you with great affection.

Raghupati Kinhi Bahut Badai
Tum Mam Priye Bharat hi Sam Bhai

Meaning:

Lord Rama praised you with great affection and declared that you were as dear to him as his beloved brother Bharata.

Sahas Badan Tumharo Yash Gaave
Us Kahi Shripati Kanth Lagaave

Meaning:

Lord Rama proclaimed that even a thousand mouths would sing praises of your greatness. Having said this, the Lord of Lakshmi lovingly embraced you.

Sankadik Brahmadi Muneesa
Narad Sarad Sahit Aheesa

Meaning:

The great sages Sanaka and his brothers, Lord Brahma, revered spiritual seers, Narada, Goddess Saraswati and Shesha all sing of your divine greatness.

Yam Kuber Digpal Jahan Te
Kavi Kobid Kahi Sake Kahan Te

Meaning:

Even Yama, Kubera, the guardians of the directions and the most accomplished poets and scholars cannot fully express the extent of your greatness.

Tum Upkar Sugreevahin Keenha
Ram Milaye Rajpad Deenha

Meaning:

You rendered a great service to Sugriva by bringing him into contact with Lord Rama. With Rama's assistance, Sugriva eventually regained his kingdom.

Tumharo Mantra Vibheeshan Mana
Lankeshwar Bhaye Sub Jag Jana

Meaning:

Vibhishana accepted and acted upon your advice, and eventually became the ruler of Lanka. His rise to the throne is known throughout the world.

Yug Sahastra Jojan Par Bhanu
Leelyo Tahi Madhur Phal Janu

Meaning:

As a child, you saw the Sun shining in the sky and mistook it for a delicious fruit. You then leapt towards it, hoping to catch and eat it.

Prabhu Mudrika Meli Mukh Mahee
Jaladhi Langhi Gaye Achraj Nahee

Meaning:

With Lord Rama's ring safely carried in your mouth, you crossed the enormous ocean. Considering your extraordinary abilities and devotion, such a remarkable accomplishment was no surprise.

Durgaam Kaj Jagat Ke Jete
Sugam Anugraha Tumhre Tete

Meaning:

Whatever difficult undertakings exist in this world can become manageable through your kindness, blessings and divine assistance.

Ram Dware Tum Rakhvare
Hoat Na Aagya Binu Paisare

Meaning:

You stand as the guardian of Lord Rama's divine entrance. No one can approach or enter without your permission.

Sub Sukh Lahai Tumhari Sarna
Tum Rakshak Kahu Ko Dar Na

Meaning:

Those who place themselves under your protection receive happiness, peace and well-being. When you are their guardian, they need not live in fear.

Aapan Tej Samharo Aapai
Teenhon Lok Haank Te Kanpe

Meaning:

Only you possess the ability to restrain your own immense brilliance and power. Your mighty roar is enough to make all three worlds tremble.

Bhoot pisach Nikat nahin Aavai
Mahavir Jab Naam Sunavai

Meaning:

Evil spirits, ghosts and frightening supernatural forces cannot approach when the name of the mighty Hanuman is remembered and proclaimed.

Nase Rog Harai Sab Peera
Japat Nirantar Hanumant Balbeera

Meaning:

The continuous remembrance of brave Lord Hanuman is believed to remove illness, ease suffering and bring relief from pain.

Sankat Se Hanuman Chudavee
Man Karam Vachan Dyan Jo Laave

Meaning:

Hanuman helps devotees overcome difficulties when they remember him wholeheartedly through their thoughts, deeds and words.

Sub Par Ram Tapasvee Raja
Tin Ke Kaj Sakal Tum Saja

Meaning:

Lord Rama, the supreme and spiritually disciplined king, stands above all. You faithfully fulfil every responsibility that contributes to accomplishing his divine purposes.

Aur Manorath Jo Koi Lavai
Sohi Amit Jeevan Phal Pavai

Meaning:

Whoever approaches you with sincere hopes and heartfelt wishes receives abundant blessings and the countless rewards of a meaningful life.

Charon Yug Partap Tumhara
Hai Parsidh Jagat Ujiyara

Meaning:

Your divine influence and greatness extend across all four ages, and your glorious reputation shines throughout the world.

Sadhu Sant Ke Tum Rakhware
Asur Nikandan Ram Dulare

Meaning:

You are the defender of saints and righteous people, the destroyer of demonic forces and the beloved servant of Lord Rama.

Ashta Sidhi Nav Nidhi Ke Daata
As Var Deen Janki Mata

Meaning:

You possess the divine blessing to bestow the eight Siddhis, or supernatural accomplishments, and the nine Nidhis, or treasures. This special boon was granted to you by Mother Janaki.

Ram Rasayan Tumhare Pasa
Sada Raho Raghupati Ke Dasa

Meaning:

You possess the sacred essence of devotion to Lord Rama, and you remain eternally dedicated to serving the Lord of the Raghu dynasty.

Tumhare Bhajan Ram Ko Pavai
Janam Janam Ke Dukh Bisravai

Meaning:

Through sincere worship and remembrance of you, devotees can attain the grace of Lord Rama and find relief from the sorrows carried across countless lifetimes.

Anth Kaal Raghuvir Pur Jayee
Jahan Janam Hari-Bakht Kahayee

Meaning:

At the end of life, a sincere devotee reaches the sacred abode of Lord Rama. Should they take birth again, they will continue to be known as a devoted follower of the Lord.

Aur Devta Chit Na Dharehi
Hanumanth Sei Sarve Sukh Karehi

Meaning:

When the mind is firmly devoted to Hanuman, the devotee need not seek fulfilment elsewhere, for Hanuman's worship is believed to bring every form of happiness and well-being.

Sankat Kate Mitey Sab Peera
Jo Sumirai Hanumat Balbeera

Meaning:

All kinds of troubles and suffering are overcome for those who sincerely remember the powerful and courageous Lord Hanuman.

Jai Jai Jai Hanuman Gosahin
Kripa Karahu Gurudev Ki Nahin

Meaning:

All glory to Lord Hanuman, the revered master! Please bless me with your kindness and guidance, just as a compassionate spiritual teacher blesses a devoted disciple.

Jo Sat Bar Path Kare Koi
Chutehi Bandhi Maha Sukh Hoi

Meaning:

Whoever recites the Hanuman Chalisa one hundred times with devotion is believed to become free from bondage and attain profound happiness.

Jo Yeh Padhe Hanuman Chalisa
Hoye Siddhi Sakhi Gaureesa

Meaning:

Those who read and recite the Hanuman Chalisa with sincerity attain spiritual fulfilment and divine blessings, with Lord Shiva himself standing as the witness to this assurance.

Tulsidas Sada Hari Chera
Keejai Naath Hridaye Mein Dera

Meaning:

Tulsidas considers himself an eternal servant of the Supreme Lord. O Hanuman, I humbly request you to establish your permanent dwelling within my heart.

॥Doha॥

Pavantnai Sankat Haran,
Mangal Murti Roop.
Ram Lakhan Sita Sahit,
Hridaye Basahu Sur Bhoop.

Meaning:


O divine son of the Wind God, remover of hardships and embodiment of all that is auspicious, please make your home within my heart, accompanied by Lord Rama, Lakshmana and Mother Sita.
